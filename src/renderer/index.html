Safex SOLO-SYNC Wallet

Runs on this computer and syncs from your own node. No website, no account.

The official wallet tools are needed once (about 40 MB, checked against Safex's published checksums). On a Mac this copies the tools you built on this computer. On Windows and Linux they download from Safex's release page.

    Network rewards
    
      
    
      
    The rules, as published in the Safex source
    
      
    • Minimum stake: 25,000 SFT.
    • Rewards are calculated for each 1000-block interval (about a day and a half).
    • Tokens must stay staked at least 8000 blocks (about 11 days) to collect.
    • A stake lasts 500,000 blocks (about 2 years) before it must be renewed.
    • Staking and unstaking cost a small SFX fee.
    
      
    These come from the safexcore source code, not from the wallet tool itself. The tool has the final say and will refuse anything it does not allow.
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    My listings
    
      
    Closing a listing marks it inactive on the chain, so nobody can buy it. The chain keeps the record, but the Market hides inactive listings by default. Each change is a small transaction with a network fee.

    1. Seller account
    
      
    You need one seller account in this wallet before you can list anything. Creating it locks 1,000 SFT. Your balance does not change, but those tokens cannot be spent while locked, and how long that lasts is not confirmed. Do this once.
    
      
      
      
    
    
    
    
      
    2. New listing
    
      
    Type what you want to receive for each unit. The wallet adds the network's 5% and shows the price to list. A listing is only a price: you send what the buyer paid for yourself, and the buyer has to tell you where. The chain does not.
    
      
      
      
      
      
      
    
      
    
    
  
    


  
    
    
    
      
    Swap needs a newer wallet tool
    
      
    Swapping SFT for SFX straight between two wallets needs the swap-capable wallet tool. The one installed here does not have it. Install the update that came with this version, then lock and unlock the wallet.
