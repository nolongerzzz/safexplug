Set up a view-only wallet

      
Uses the official Safex wallet tool, in view-only mode: it can see payments coming in but can never spend. You will paste your address and private view key once, into the wallet tool's own window, never into this app. Never use your seed or spend key.

      

        
        
        
      

      

      

    

    

      
This system has no one-click wallet setup. Run the official safex-wallet-rpc yourself and enter its address (normally 127.0.0.1:18082; your node's 17402 is not it).

      

        
        
      

    

    

      

      
Only your wallet's incoming payments are read. This app never asks for or stores your seed or keys.
